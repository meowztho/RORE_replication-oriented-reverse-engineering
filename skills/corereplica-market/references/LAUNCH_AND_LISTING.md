# Launch and Listing

Prepare landing/pricing/store material from Product Truth plus sourced market evidence.

## Landing

Lead with the target product's own outcome, audience and differentiation. A useful default structure is: outcome-focused hero, problem/context, how it works, differentiating capabilities, pricing, FAQ/objections and final CTA. Use real product screenshots only when they actually exist. Do not fabricate testimonials, user counts, ratings, press or customer logos.

## Pricing

Research the reference and relevant alternatives from current public pricing sources, with URL/date. Include sourced complaint evidence when it materially informs the model. Product/business pricing remains a user/business decision, not something inferred from a competitor price. Keep plan count/complexity proportional to the product; do not add tiers for appearance.

## Store/platform listing

Write fresh copy in the target brand voice. Current field limits, review policies, screenshot requirements, privacy/data-safety forms, age ratings, support/privacy URLs and submission rules can change; re-check official current platform documentation before finalizing/submitting.

The bundled `listing.py` is a deterministic lint helper for configured rules/residue. It is not authoritative proof of current App Store/Play policy compliance.

## Launch plan

Define the smallest credible beta/launch path, analytics/error tracking that respects the product's privacy posture, likely communities/channels grounded in research, and concrete feedback collection from early users. Do not manufacture proof before it exists.
